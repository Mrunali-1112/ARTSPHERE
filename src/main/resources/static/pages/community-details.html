About This Guild

Creative Souls is a welcoming space for artists of every discipline and craft. We come together to share works in progress, critique each other's explorations constructively, organize community jams, and discover lifelong collaboration partners.

Guild Code of Conduct

  • Be kind, generous, and constructive in all feedback.
  • Post only authentic, original creations and cite references.
  • Encourage emerging artists and beginners warmly.
  • Zero tolerance for spam, harassment, or unsolicited sales.
  • Keep discussions focused on craft, process, and creative growth.
