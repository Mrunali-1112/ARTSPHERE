About This Event

This hands-on workshop will introduce you to the world of watercolors. You will learn basic tools, color mixing, different brush techniques, and create your own small artwork by the end of the session. No prior experience is needed — just bring your creativity!

What You'll Learn

  • Introduction to watercolor materials
  • Color mixing and blending techniques
  • Brush control and texture creation
  • Step-by-step guided painting
  • Tips and tricks from an experienced artist

Who Can Join?
