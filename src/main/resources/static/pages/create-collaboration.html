Co-Creation Guidelines

  • Shared Artistic Attribution

    All collaborators must receive clear, uncompromised credit in final releases.

  • Transparent Expectations

    State your timeline, milestones, and whether compensation/stipend is offered.

  • Independent & Respectful

    ArtSphere is a space for mutual artistic growth without corporate gatekeeping.

How to Attract Great Partners

Creators receive 3x more relevant responses when they attach a moodboard link and clearly define their artistic role versus what they need help with.

  • Be specific about your vision
  • Share visual references
  • Mention skills and roles clearly
  • Set a realistic timeline
  • Be open and collaborative

Tips for a Strong Pitch

  1. 1 Write a clear and concise headline that grabs attention.
  2. 2 Include a moodboard or reference link if possible.
  3. 3 Be transparent about your needs, timeline, and compensation.
  4. 4 Show what makes your project unique and meaningful.
