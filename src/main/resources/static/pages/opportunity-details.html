Serendipity Arts Residency 2026

Serendipity Arts Foundation Panaji, Goa • In-Person Studio Residency
COMPENSATION / GRANT ₹1,50,000 Stipend + Studio

DISCIPLINE Multidisciplinary
SELECTION COHORT 6 Fellows Selected
✦

About the Opportunity

The Serendipity Arts Residency invites applications from experimental multidisciplinary practitioners across South Asia. The 6-week residency provides dedicated studio space in Goa, mentorship from internationally acclaimed curators, and a full production budget to realize a site-specific installation.

✦

Deliverables & Support Provided

  • Private 400 sq.ft individual studio workspace with high-speed internet and natural lighting.
  • ₹1,50,000 living stipend distributed across two milestones.
  • Up to ₹75,000 material and fabrication budget reimbursed upon approved receipts.
  • Featured showcase during the Serendipity Arts Festival open week.
✦

Eligibility Criteria

Open to practitioners of visual arts, animation, contemporary dance, sound design, and experimental writing with at least 2 years of active practice. Students currently enrolled in degree programs are not eligible.
✦

Host Organization

Serendipity Arts Foundation

One of South Asia's largest multi-arts initiatives, fostering cultural exchange, artistic research, and community-engaged public art.

✦

Key Timeline

Applications Close Oct 30, 2026
Jury Shortlist Nov 10, 2026
Residency Starts Nov 20, 2026
✦

Fair Practice Guarantee

Artists retain 100% intellectual property ownership of work produced during the program. ArtSphere verifies all host organizations prior to publication.
