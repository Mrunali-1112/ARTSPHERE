Artistic Inquiry & Methodology

"My practice centers on capturing atmospheric stillness within urban environments. By combining loose digital brushwork with analog print textures, I explore themes of memory, transience, and the poetic geometry of South Asian cities. I am constantly open to cross-disciplinary projects with sound designers, animators, and poets."
