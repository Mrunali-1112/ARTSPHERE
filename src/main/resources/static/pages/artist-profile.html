Artistic Inquiry & Methodology

“My practice centers on exploring atmospheric stillness within urban and domestic environments. By combining loose digital brushwork with textured, tactile sensibilities, I explore themes of memory, transience, and the poetic geometry of everyday spaces. I am constantly open to cross-disciplinary projects with musicians, animators, sound designers, and poets.”
